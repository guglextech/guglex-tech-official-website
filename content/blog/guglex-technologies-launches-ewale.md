Ewale is live. It is the consumer product we built so people in Ghana can pay for everyday services without switching between portals, agents, and half-finished checkout flows.

You can use it on the web at [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com), or from any phone on **\*714\*22#**.

## What shipped

Ewale covers the purchases people actually make every week:

- **Airtime** on MTN, Telecel, and AirtelTigo
- **Data bundles** from a live list, not a hardcoded menu
- **ECG prepaid** — pay a meter, receive a token
- **WASSCE result checkers** — vouchers delivered after settlement

Each of those is a complete flow: validate the destination, collect payment, confirm the outcome, and show the customer what happened.

## Why we built it

The services exist. The friction is in how they are sold. Different websites, unclear confirmation, channels that work on one network and fail on another, and experiences that assume a smartphone and a stable connection.

We wanted one product that is fast when the network is good, and still usable when it is not.

USSD is not a fallback for us. It is a first-class surface. A large share of Ghana still pays from a feature phone or from a smartphone with no data. If the product only lives in a browser, it is incomplete.

## How to use it

**On the web.** Open [ewalepay.com](https://ewalepay.com), pick a service, enter the details, and approve the payment prompt on your phone.

**On USSD.** Dial **\*714\*22#**, follow the menu, and confirm with your mobile money PIN.

You do not need an app.
